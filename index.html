About

From partner programs to the pipeline they create

I started in programs and alliances at Veeam in 2014. Over eleven years I grew with the company into running EMEA alliance marketing for six strategic alliances and a wider partner portfolio, then EMEA events and enterprise marketing.

At Pathlock I moved to a global role and built the partner marketing function from scratch. In both companies I backed the partners, events and accounts that created pipeline, tracked every one of them, and stopped funding the ones that didn't.

I work as a team lead or hands-on, and I'm comfortable in global teams that run around the clock.

Partner enablementVeeam · Partner sales · Global · 2022–25

A three-level sales program for 1,500+ partner sellers

I built a sales enablement framework for partner sales teams. It took sellers from qualifying a customer to winning over the whole buying committee, with each level pairing online theory courses with live, trainer-led simulations in partner offices.

Program levels3
FormatOnline + live
Partner sellers trained1,500+
How it worked
  • Level 1, Qualifying: asking the right questions and steering the conversation to uncover the customer's need for the product.
  • Level 2, Pitching: using the qualification sheet to build a pitch that earns a demo or a deeper conversation.
  • Level 3, The buying committee: the typical roles, each role's decision factors, and how to build and land a proposition that works for all or most of them.
  • Online theory courses came first, followed by live trainer-led simulations held in partner offices across multiple global locations.
  • More than 1,500 partner sellers completed at least one module, and most completed two or more.
  • As far as I know, the program is still running at Veeam today.
